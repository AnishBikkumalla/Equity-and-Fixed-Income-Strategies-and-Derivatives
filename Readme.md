# Equity and Fixed Income Strategies and Derivatives

This repository features a comprehensive investment strategy analysis covering equity markets, fixed-income liability matching, and derivative risk management[cite: 2]. It combines quantitative Excel modeling with qualitative strategic recommendations to form an integrated portfolio approach[cite: 2].

## Repository Contents

*   **`-Equity and Fixed Income Strategies and Derivatives-Bikkumalla anish.docx`**: The primary research report detailing the methodologies, valuation models, and strategic recommendations[cite: 2].
*   **`Equity and Fixed Income Strategies and Derivatives Bikkumalla anish Inbox .xlsx`**: The companion Excel workbook containing the working formulas for the Dividend Discount Model (DDM), bond duration/convexity calculations, and the Black-Scholes-Merton (BSM) options pricing model.

## Project Overview

### 1. Equity Investment Strategy
The project conducts absolute and relative valuations on three major U.S. consumer retail corporations: Walmart (WMT), Target (TGT), and Costco (COST)[cite: 2]. 
*   **Absolute Valuation:** Utilizes the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) and a two-stage Dividend Discount Model (DDM) to estimate intrinsic value[cite: 2]. 
*   **Relative Valuation:** Compares Price-to-Earnings (P/E) and Enterprise Value-to-EBITDA (EV/EBITDA) multiples against a peer group of discount retailers[cite: 2]. 
*   **Conclusion:** Identifies that standard DDM often undervalues low-payout, buyback-oriented retailers, recommending a moderately-active, core-satellite methodology to navigate valuation disparities[cite: 2].

### 2. Fixed Income Investment Strategy
The fixed-income section addresses a liability-driven investing scenario to fund a \$1,000,000 obligation due in five years[cite: 2]. 
*   **Analysis:** Compares two 15% annual-coupon corporate bonds (5-year vs. 10-year maturity) across a flat 10% yield curve[cite: 2].
*   **Metrics:** Calculates Macaulay Duration, Modified Duration, and Convexity to evaluate interest rate sensitivity and reinvestment risk[cite: 2].
*   **Conclusion:** Recommends a passive immunization strategy using the 5-year bond to tightly match the liability horizon and minimize price risk[cite: 2].

### 3. Derivatives and Risk Management
The project designs an event-driven options strategy centered around the volatility of the 2020 U.S. Presidential Election[cite: 2]. 
*   **Strategy:** Implements a long straddle on the SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY) to capitalize on high outcome uncertainty and bimodal market risks[cite: 2]. 
*   **Pricing:** Utilizes the Black-Scholes-Merton (BSM) model to price the at-the-money call and put options[cite: 2]. 
*   **Outcome:** The realized post-election volatility generated an 8.93 net profit per straddle, yielding a 46.0% return on the invested premium[cite: 2].
